Your taste code

Claude can pick films for your flight from what the airline is showing this month. Give it your IMDb or Letterboxd history once and it skips what you've seen and ranks the rest by how you score films. This page turns your export into a short code you paste into the chat.

If your IMDb ratings are public, you can now skip this page and paste your profile link into the chat instead. The upload below still works when the link doesn't, or for a Letterboxd export.

1. Add your export

    IMDb: on a computer, signed in, open imdb.com/list/ratings/export. The file shows up under Your exports a minute later. For the watchlist, use the three-dot menu on your Watchlist page.

    Letterboxd: Settings, Data, Export your data. Drop the zip in as it is.

    2. Check it

    0rated
    0seen
    0watchlist

    Your files stay in this browser. The code stores the film ids and your scores, nothing else, for a year. Anyone who has the code can see that list.

    3. Paste it into Claude

    Keep it somewhere you'll find it again, a note on your phone works. Next flight, paste the same code.

    hours

    Flying United to Chicago tomorrow, 9 hours, no idea what's on. Show me what's good and ask me what I've seen.

    First time? Set up the Claude app

    Once, on claude.ai in a browser. It then works in the phone app too.

    1. Customize, Plugins, Add, Add marketplace, and enter len5ky/seatback-cinema. Install Seatback Cinema. That's it.
    2. Optional: on the plugin's Connectors tab, add the Seatback Cinema connector (no login). It lets you paste an IMDb or Letterboxd export straight into the chat.
    3. No plugins on your plan? Download the skill zip and upload it, unopened, at Customize, Skills.
    Seatback Cinema: monthly in-flight film catalogues for 11 airlines. Privacy.
