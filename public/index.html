    Notes

    • Delta, Turkish Airlines and Air France do not publish a full catalogue: Delta shows a few dozen titles on marketing pages, Turkish ten highlights, Air France nothing. American Airlines and Qatar Airways are being added.
    • "Matched" means a high- or medium-confidence match on title, year, director and runtime. Low-confidence guesses are left out. Emirates sends no release year, so its regional-language films match least often.
    • A catalogue can differ by route, aircraft and cabin. Qantas is the large international seatback catalogue and lists audio-described versions as separate entries.
    • Median and mean IMDb rating are blank until the export carries ratings.
    • Films are counted per catalogue entry as the airline lists them. Data is refreshed on the 1st and 15th of each month. IMDb data is used under its non-commercial licence.
